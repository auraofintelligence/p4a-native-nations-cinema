# Native Nations of the World: Sovereign Co-Evolution Lab

A public-source, static, offline-friendly lab in the P4A cinema family, addressed to native nations of the world: Indigenous data sovereignty, decentralised compute, reciprocity economics (the C-Hour) and the long road toward revised agreements, offered as pathways to consider, never directions to follow.

**Live site:** https://auraofintelligence.github.io/p4a-native-nations-cinema/

**README reviewed 25 November 2026.** Current source revision: 1 November 2026 (`63bda54`). The July instruments and hardware research retain their own dates; this review does not independently reverify treaty status, legal interpretation or engineering claims. The lab is an invitation, not an Indigenous representative body or a membership register.

## Three visible registers

Everything on the site is colour-coded by provenance, and never mixed:

- **Gold panels, the established record.** Real instruments, quoted exactly and cited: UNDRIP, FPIC, ILO Convention 169, the Nagoya Protocol, the WIPO treaty on genetic resources, the CARE Principles, the First Nations principles of OCAP®, the Maiam nayri Wingara principles, Te Mana Raraunga, Local Contexts TK & BC Labels, and a world-edition treaty atlas.
- **Purple panels, P4A proposals.** Rapid iterations from one workbench: the Community-Hour (a receipt for care, mobilised never monetised, the site's declared purpose, with a one-page legislative ask), the heart-first civic surge, the L0–L3 sovereign compute gradient, and a kintsugi protocol for repairing broken agreements. They contain errors by the nature of their speed. React, revise, refuse, all three are welcome.
- **Ember panels, culture.** Song doorways from the author's own music (Shifting Sands of Timeless Redlands, Kintsugi Protocol, and the Starseed Code hymns). Neither record nor proposal: the resonance layer, each naming its maker.

## Reading the record without surrendering authority

The [rights instruments room](pages/instruments.html) distinguishes who held the pen: Indigenous-authored frameworks, instruments negotiated or fought into being through international processes, and state-authored instruments. Each nation's own law and authority are not derived from these documents. Quoted instruments and the author's interpretation are distinct; dated ratification and legal status need fresh official verification.

The [data sovereignty room](pages/data-sovereignty.html) connects collective benefit, authority, responsibility, ethics, ownership/control/access/possession and culturally governed labels with local data and AI. These frameworks have their own authors, communities and applicability. Their inclusion is not permission to collect Indigenous knowledge or train models on it.

The [treaty atlas](pages/treaty-atlas.html) is a source and agreement shelf, not a list of enrolled nations, and its public institution references do not imply consent. The [reciprocity treaty](pages/reciprocity-treaty.html) and [Kintsugi protocol](pages/seams.html) are proposals for repair, accountability and visible provenance, not an adopted agreement.

## C-Hour, sovereign compute and community choice

The [C-Hour room](pages/c-hour.html) centres the equality of one verified human hour. The current connected [C-Hour framework](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/C-Hour-introduction/) specifies voluntary contribution only, no monetary equivalent, no market exchange, and separate records for paid work, money and machine activity. Each community decides what contribution means under its own law and protocols; refusal remains valid.

The [compute room](pages/compute.html) describes a July four-level example, from personal devices to shared local systems, community racks and external computing. It is an illustrative hardware gradient, not a mandatory hierarchy or a verified deployment. The wider current design treats L0 as the person's private environment and shared spaces as flexible, permissioned arrangements. Hardware figures and resilience assumptions need fresh engineering and local review.

The [surge](pages/surge.html) and [abundance](pages/abundance.html) rooms explore community capability, care, creativity, resilience and long-term futures. No legal adoption, funding, treatment, installation or affiliation follows from the proposals.

## Standing commitments

- This site never speaks for any nation, and lists no nation except by that nation's own hand.
- Free, prior and informed consent is treated as minimum viable respect, including the right to refuse.
- Any nation's entry is published verbatim, and removed on request without process.
- No analytics, no accounts, no cookies, no forms. Static pages only.
- Correction of anything, for any reason, is one message away: open an issue.

## Self-onboarding

Nations and peoples not visible here can choose their own path, see the [onboarding room](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/p4a-native-nations-cinema/pages/onboard.html), the entry template inside it, and [ONBOARD_WITH_AN_AGENT.md](ONBOARD_WITH_AN_AGENT.md) for building your own version with your own AI assistant. Declining is a first-class outcome.

The onboarding room supports a nation-authored entry, an independent version built with its own AI and hosting, adaptation of selected modules, or refusal. No existing people are enrolled by the site. No accounts, forms, analytics or automatic follow-up are introduced. A nation's own authority decides whether material may appear; preserve the original self-description and correction/removal rights. Public Git history can retain earlier published material, so restricted knowledge should never be put in a public entry in the first place.

**Nation-controlled onboarding is not P4A political membership.** The localised website prompt expressly says it is not a membership funnel. The Australian membership guide is a separate optional public resource for people who independently seek it. Do not replace this onboarding room, prefill an application, route nation entries into the CRM, collect identity/cultural data, or imply endorsement.
